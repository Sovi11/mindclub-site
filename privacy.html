mindclub

Privacy

Covers the Mindclub website and the Mindclub browser extension. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Short version. Mindclub keeps your puzzle results so you and the people you follow can compare them. The extension only reads text that a game page copies when you tap its Share button, and only keeps it if it's a game result. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

The website

What we store

  • Your account: your email address if you sign in with email (guest accounts have none), plus the display name and @handle you choose and which games you show on Today.
  • Your results: for each puzzle you log, the game, the day, the result as shown (for example 3/6), a score used for ranking, the puzzle number, and an optional star rating.
  • Your connections: who you follow, challenges you send or receive (with any note you add), and game suggestions and votes.

Who can see it

  • Your name and handle are visible to people you follow, people who follow you, people you've challenged, and anyone who searches for your handle.
  • People who follow you see your results. A score stays hidden from them until they've logged the same puzzle, so nobody is spoiled.
  • Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Your clipboard

Pasting a result is always your choice. If you turn on auto-log, the site reads your clipboard once when you come back to the tab. If the text isn't a game result it is ignored and never leaves your browser.

In your browser

The site keeps a few preferences in your browser's local storage (for example whether auto-log is on, or that you hid the demo friend). They stay on your device.

The browser extension

What it reads

The extension runs only on the game sites it supports (NYT Games, LinkedIn Games, Krillion, Merriam-Webster's Quordle and Octordle, Waffle, Worldle, Globle, Framed and Nerdle) and on the Mindclub site. On a game site it looks at text the page copies to the clipboard or shares, which is what a Share button does. It checks that text inside your browser: if it isn't a game result, it is dropped right there and never stored or sent. It does not read your browsing history, other tabs, other sites, or anything you type.

What it sends

When the text is a game result, the extension sends Mindclub the game, the day, the result, its score, the puzzle number, and your extension key, so the result is added to your account. Nothing else.

What it keeps on your device

  • Your extension key. You get it when you click Connect on Mindclub. Mindclub stores only a scrambled (hashed) copy. The key can only add a result to your own account for yesterday, today or tomorrow, and can never change or delete one.
  • A short list of the last results it logged, shown in its popup.

Disconnecting

Disconnect in the extension's popup to forget the key in that browser. Disconnect extension on Mindclub also cancels every key for your account. Removing the extension deletes what it kept.

Where it lives

Accounts and results are stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India. The website is served by GitHub Pages, with its fonts from Google Fonts and one code library from jsDelivr. Those services may see your IP address when they serve files, as any website host does.

Keeping and deleting

  • You can remove any result from My history at any time, and unfollow or remove followers on People.
  • Guest accounts that haven't been used for 30 days are deleted automatically, with everything in them.
  • To delete your whole account, open an issue at github.com/Sovi11/mindclub-site/issues asking for deletion. Don't post your email there: just give your @handle, and we'll reply with how to confirm the account is yours before anything is deleted.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/Sovi11/mindclub-site/issues. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
